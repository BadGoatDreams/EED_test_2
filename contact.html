Our Leadership

An all-volunteer team guided by an Indigenous-majority board

SW
Sara Wecker
Founder & Executive Director

Sara founded Essential Eats Distributors in 2017 after learning a food pantry serving families near the Blackfeet Nation had shut down before the holidays. She assembled a truckload of Christmas boxes — 75 to 100 boxes distributed in 15 minutes — and recognized the scale of unmet need. She leads a team of approximately eight women focused on grassroots organizing and fundraising.

SEH
Samuel Enemy-Hunter
Board Member, Crow Nation

Enrolled member of the Crow Nation from Lodge Grass, Montana. Samuel has been instrumental in expanding EED's bike donation program to the Crow Nation, describing bikes as a modern replacement for horses: "You're taking what we would have had as tribal people — horses — and replacing that with bikes so they can get around."

AD
Andrea Davis
Board Member, Mayor of Missoula

Missoula voters elected Andrea Davis as mayor in November 2023. She serves on the EED board, supporting the organization's mission of reducing food insecurity across Montana's tribal lands.

Our board is majority Indigenous — composed of enrolled Tribal members with representation from each nation we serve.

Essential Eats Distributors has no paid staff. All officers, board members, and volunteers donate their time. We coordinate operations through phone, email, and social media without any physical facility overhead.
