Essential Eats Distributors

A volunteer-based virtual nonprofit providing resources to Indigenous communities across Montana. Founded in 2017 by Sara Wecker, we work with tribal leaders to deliver fresh, culturally appropriate food and essential items directly to those in need. All-volunteer, no paid staff. Majority-Indigenous board.

Our Services Get in Touch

Our Mission

Serving communities with dignity, compassion, and reliability

Community First

We put the needs of our communities at the center of everything we do, building trust through consistent and caring service.

Nutritious Meals

Providing balanced, healthy food options to families who need them most, because everyone deserves a wholesome meal.

Lasting Impact

Creating sustainable programs that empower individuals and strengthen the social fabric of the areas we serve.

What We Do

A snapshot of our core activities

Food Distribution

Organizing regular food distribution events across Montana reservations and underserved areas.

Community Events

Hosting gatherings that bring people together, fostering connection and mutual support.

Resource Navigation

Helping families connect with housing, healthcare, and other vital community resources.

Bike Donation Program

Annual bike donations providing transportation and recreation to youth on Blackfeet and Fort Belknap reservations.

Food Sovereignty Initiative

Developing long-term Indigenous agricultural infrastructure, including plans for a cattle herd on tribal lands.
